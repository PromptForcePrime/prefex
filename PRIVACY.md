# Promptforce.ai Privacy Policy

**Effective July 8, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **DayTwoAI LLC**, a Texas limited liability
company doing business as **Promptforce.ai** ("**we**," "**us**"), handles
information in connection with the **Prefex** software (the "**Software**") and
the **promptforce.ai** website (the "**Site**").

Our guiding principle: **Prefex is self-hosted and runs on infrastructure you
control. We do not operate a cloud service in your request path, and we do not
receive your prompts, responses, or source code.**

---

## 1. Prefex Software — what stays local

When you run the Prefex Software:

- Your prompts and responses flow from your client, through your self-hosted
  proxy, to the third-party LLM provider **you** configure (e.g., Anthropic,
  OpenAI, Google). They are handled under **that provider's** privacy policy, not
  ours. We never see or receive them.
- Operational data — request metadata (token counts, model, latency, cost, cache
  indicators, hashes) and, depending on your configuration, locally retained
  items such as recoverable compacted-output originals, extracted session-memory
  facts, an opt-in advisor corpus, your configuration, and your license
  metadata — is stored **only on infrastructure you control**. You can inspect
  and delete it at any time. See `TRUST.md` for specifics.

We do not collect this operational data. The Software sends nothing to us
automatically.

---

## 2. Information we may receive

We only receive information on **opt-in, user-initiated** paths:

- **Leaderboard submission (optional):** if you choose to submit to the public
  leaderboard from the dashboard, we receive aggregate statistics (such as
  request counts and savings totals — never prompt or response content) plus the
  **username and email you enter** so your entry can be attributed.
- **Marketplace catalog fetch (optional):** if you enable telemetry, the Software
  downloads an optional catalog from our endpoint. This is a download; it does not
  upload your usage data.
- **Website & contact:** if you visit the Site or email us (e.g.,
  contact@daytwoai.com), we receive standard web-server/log information and any
  details you choose to provide. The Site may use basic, privacy-respecting
  analytics and essential cookies.
- **License issuance:** if you request a license key, we receive the information
  you provide for that request (such as your email and tier). License
  **verification** happens offline on your machine and requires no call to us.

---

## 3. How we use information

We use the limited information above to operate the leaderboard, respond to your
inquiries, issue and support licenses, and maintain and improve the Software and
Site. **We do not sell your personal information**, and we do not use your
prompts, responses, or source code for any purpose (we never receive them).

---

## 4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service
providers who help us operate the Site and communications (e.g., hosting, email),
under confidentiality obligations, and as required by law or to protect our
rights. Publicly submitted leaderboard entries are, by design, public.

---

## 5. Retention

We retain the limited information we receive only as long as needed for the
purpose described (e.g., to maintain a leaderboard entry or answer your inquiry)
or as required by law. Operational data created by the Software stays on your
infrastructure and is controlled and deleted by you.

---

## 6. Your choices

- Keep telemetry disabled (the default) to limit outbound connections to your
  configured LLM provider only.
- Don't submit to the leaderboard if you'd rather not share a username/email.
- Contact us at **contact@daytwoai.com** to ask about, correct, or delete
  information you've provided to us. Depending on where you live, you may have
  additional rights under applicable law; we will honor them as required.

---

## 7. Children

The Software and Site are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age
in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect their information.

---

## 8. International

We operate from the United States. If you contact us or submit information from
outside the U.S., you understand it may be processed in the U.S.

---

## 9. Changes

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the
effective date above and, where appropriate, a notice on the Site.
